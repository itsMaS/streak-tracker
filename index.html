How it works

    
    
      
  • 💪Log a workout every day to grow the streak. Adding activities (plank, run, ride…) with how much you did is optional.
    • 
      
  • 🧠Migraine day: the streak is kept safe but doesn't grow. You can still log a workout on a migraine day — then it counts.
    • 
      
  • Recovery day: the day after a migraine can be marked as recovery. Like a migraine day it keeps the streak safe without growing it. One per migraine.
    • 
      
  • Migraine tab: optional details (severity, meds, triggers, notes), patterns, a doctor report you can save as PDF, and a backup file. Details stay on this phone and are never shared with buddies.
    • 
      
  • 📅Tap any past day in the calendar or week row to add, change, or clear it.
    • 
      
  • 📈The graph shows plank duration over time — it appears once you've logged a plank. Tap a dot for details.
    • 
      
  • 🏅Milestones unlock at 3, 7, 14, 21, 30, 50, 75 and 100 days.
    • 
      
  • 🪙Logging a workout earns coins: 10 × your streak, up to 30×. Only today's log pays out (once per day) — backfilled days keep the streak honest but earn nothing.
    • 
      
  • 🚜Spend coins on farm animals at the bottom. Some unlock once your best streak reaches 7, 14 or 30 days, and every extra copy of an animal costs 25% more. They wander when today is logged and doze when it isn't.
    • 
      
  • 💛Buddies: swap codes with friends to see each other's streaks, calendars, planks and farms — and send cheers 📣 or nudges 👉.
    • 
      
  • 🐾The buddies wander by sometimes. Tap them — they like it.
    • 
      
  • 😡Angry raccoon: flip it on in Settings and a very cross raccoon appears on your lock screen at reminder time if today is still empty — even with the app closed.
